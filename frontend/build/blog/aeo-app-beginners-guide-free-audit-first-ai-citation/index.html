AEO-APP Beginner’s Guide: From Free Audit to Your First AI Engine Citation

AEO-APP Beginner’s Guide: From Free Audit to Your First AI Engine Citation

AEO App is an automated content creation and posting tool that helps your business reach more people through AI search engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews.

It checks your website and your competitors’ online presence, finds out what your customers are searching for, and helps you create relevant content. You can also publish this content across platforms such as Google Business Profile, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and LinkedIn.

The first step is to understand how visible your business is in AI search through a free audit. This guide explains how to get started, use the platform, and improve your chances of getting your first AI engine citation.

What Is AEO and Why Your Business Needs It Now

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of preparing your website and online content so that AI-powered search engines are more likely to include information about your business in their answers when people ask questions.

Traditional SEO focuses on ranking your website high in Google search results. AEO has a slightly different goal. It aims to get your brand, website, or content included in answers when people ask questions using AI tools such as ChatGPT.

For example, if someone asks ChatGPT, "Where should I book a resort near a beach?", it would help your business if the answer included your resort's name or a link to your website.

Many people now use tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews to find information and choose products and services. So, focusing only on Google search results is no longer enough. You also need to pay attention to how your business appears in AI search.

This is where the AEO App can help. It brings several activities together on one platform, from finding the questions customers ask to creating and publishing relevant content.

How AEO App Works: The Four-Step Process

The AEO App breaks down the AEO process into four main steps.

Step 1: Audit & Discover

First, enter your website URL in AEO App. The platform then provides a free audit by reviewing your website and your competitors’ online presence.

The audit mainly looks at three things:

  • Your existing website content and structure.
  • How your competitors present themselves online.
  • The questions your customers ask on Google and AI search engines.

Based on this information, you will receive an AI Visibility Score out of 100, showing your business's current position. You can also identify topics that your website should cover but does not have content for yet. These are known as Content Gaps.

Step 2: Create Content

Once the audit is complete, AEO App helps you generate different types of content—including images, video reels, social media posts, and written captions—to answer specific customer questions.

You can choose the questions that matter most to your business. AEO App then uses your Company Profile and Brand Kit to prepare content that matches your business's style and identity.

For example, imagine the audit identifies the topic "automated content posting social media tool". From that one question, AEO App can create five different content pieces ready to publish.

Step 3: Publish Across Channels

Once your content is ready, you can connect your Google Business Profile, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and LinkedIn accounts to AEO App. After that, the platform publishes content across these channels according to the schedule you set.

You do not need to log in to every platform separately to publish posts. You can manage the entire process through the AEO App and review each post in the Social Media Calendar before it goes live.

Step 4: Track Visibility

Publishing content alone is not enough. Through the AEO App dashboard, you can track how often your brand is mentioned across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews.

You can see how often your business appears in AI answers, how your Share of Voice compares with competitors, and how many times AI tools link to your website.

Your Free Audit: What You’ll Learn and How to Use It

A Free Audit is one of the easiest ways to understand where your business currently stands in AI search. To get started, enter your website URL, and AEO App will prepare an audit report.

This report helps you understand several things:

  • AI Visibility Score: See how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers.
  • Customer Questions: See how many important questions related to your business already mention your brand.
  • Competitor Comparison: Understand the differences between your content and your competitors' content.
  • High-Intent Questions: Identify the customer questions that are most relevant to your business.

You can also see the Monthly Search Volume and Search Intent for each question. This makes it easier to decide which topics you should create content for first.

For example, if the audit shows that AI recommends a competitor's business for a particular question while your brand does not appear, you can give that topic more attention by creating clear, useful content that answers it.

AEO App also helps you find relevant topics and understand which channels, such as Google Business Profile, Instagram, and LinkedIn, deserve more attention. After the audit, AEO App provides a content calendar with posts you can publish over the next seven days, helping you know what to create and when to publish, even if you are just getting started.

From Audit to Your First AI Engine Citation: The Week-One Sprint

Here is how to get started with the AEO App during your first week.

Day 1–2: Choose Your Questions and Create Content

First, select the questions that matter most to your business from the Prompts list. Then, click the Rank Me High option.

Based on your audit data, the AEO App will prepare your first batch of content—including reels, carousel posts, captions, and Google Business Profile updates—focused on those main customer questions.

Day 3–4: Publish Your Content

Next, publish the prepared content across your connected social media accounts.

Once your content is available on Google Business Profile and other social media platforms, people can start seeing it, and search engines and AI tools can discover it.

However, publishing content doesn't guarantee Google will index it immediately, or that AI engines will cite it. The time taken to discover and use content can vary across platforms.

Day 5–7: Track Your Results

After publishing your first batch of content, use our App to track your brand mentions.

AI tools such as Perplexity often provide answers with links to their sources, creating an opportunity for new and clearly written content to be discovered.

In your next analysis, check whether your AI Visibility Score or the number of links to your website has changed. While you may not receive your first citation within the first week, this period lets you understand the publishing process and build a routine for future activities.

Core Features That Drive AI Visibility

AEO App includes several features that can help improve your business's visibility in AI search:

  • One-Question, Five-Posts Model: When you select a customer question, AEO App prepares five different pieces of content from five different angles. Instead of repeating the same information in every post, you cover different aspects of the question, making more information about your business available online.

  • Multi-Channel Distribution: Share the same content across Google Business Profile, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and LinkedIn. This saves time because you do not have to upload content separately to each platform.

  • Automated Calendar and Publishing: AEO App helps you prepare a content calendar, schedule posts, and manage publishing so you do not have to decide every day when to post. You can also review the content before it goes live.

  • Competitor and Website Strength Tracking: Understand which competitors are being recommended in AI answers and track website strength metrics such as Domain Authority to evaluate your online presence.

  • AI Visibility Tracking: The AEO App dashboard displays your AI Visibility Score, Share of Voice, and the number of links AI tools provide to your website. You can also check which customer questions mention your business and which content helps you appear in those answers.

Getting Started: Next Steps and Measuring Success

To get started with AEO App, visit aeo-app.com and enter your website URL to complete a Free Audit.

Once you receive the audit, decide how often you want to publish content. AEO App pricing is structured around your publishing frequency, with options to publish once a week, several times a week, or every day. You do not have to start by publishing every day; you can begin with one relevant topic, prepare useful content, and publish it once a week.

After your first week, check a few important details on your AEO App dashboard:

  • Has your AI Visibility Score changed?
  • How does your Share of Voice compare with your competitors?
  • How many times have AI tools linked to your website?
  • Has your Domain Authority changed?

Each analysis is saved in History so that you can compare your progress over time with the results from your first audit.

The main purpose of using AEO App is not just to publish content once, but to keep answering the questions customers ask across more platforms. Over time, this regular publishing may improve your chances of gaining visibility in AI search.

So, start with a Free Audit. Understand what your business needs, identify the right topics, and begin preparing content for the next week.
