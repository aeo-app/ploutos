Every quarter we pull visibility data across a rotating panel of sites and look for what actually shifted. Q1 2026's numbers are a little more interesting than usual.

## The headline number

Average AI-answer visibility across the panel rose 6.2 percentage points quarter over quarter — but almost all of that gain came from one segment: sites that shipped structured FAQ schema in the quarter, not sites that simply added more content.

## By industry

| Industry | Avg visibility (Q1) | QoQ change |
|---|---|---|
| SaaS | 34% | +8.1pp |
| DTC | 21% | +2.4pp |
| Fintech | 29% | +5.7pp |

SaaS sites saw the largest jump, largely concentrated in companies that restructured their documentation into direct question-and-answer pairs rather than long-form guides.

## What moved the needle, and what didn't

**Moved it**: FAQ schema markup, direct-answer paragraph structure, and — somewhat surprisingly — reducing average page length. Shorter, more focused pages outperformed comprehensive "ultimate guide" pages on citation rate.

**Didn't move it**: raw word count, backlink volume, and traditional keyword density. None of these correlated meaningfully with AI citation rate this quarter, even though they still matter for traditional search.

We'll keep tracking this panel through Q2 — if the FAQ-schema effect holds, it's worth prioritizing over most other on-page work right now.
