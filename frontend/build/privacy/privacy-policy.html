At AEO App, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your details when you use the AEO App.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use the AEO App:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, or login credentials provided during registration.
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, device type, operating system version, and app activity data to improve user experience.
  • Permissions (if applicable): Camera, storage, or location access, only with user approval.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the features of the AEO App.
  • To create and manage your user account.
  • To send important updates, notifications, and customer support responses.
  • To improve app performance, security, and user experience.

3. Connected Social Media Accounts

You can connect your LinkedIn Company Page, Google Business Profile, YouTube channel, Facebook Page or Instagram account to the AEO App so that we can publish the posts you create and approve. You connect an account by signing in on that platform's own page and choosing what to allow. We never see your password for these platforms.

  • What we receive: an access token from the platform, basic profile information such as your name, and the names and IDs of the pages, business locations or channels you manage, so you can choose where to publish.
  • How we use it: only to publish and schedule the posts, images and videos that you create or approve in the AEO App, to the page, location or channel you select. We do not send messages on your behalf or publish anything you have not approved.
  • Disconnecting: you can disconnect any account at any time from Manage accounts in the app. When you do, we delete the stored access token for that account. You can also remove the AEO App's access from the platform's own settings, for example Google's third-party access page.

Google user data: The AEO App's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube: The AEO App uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the channel you connect. By connecting YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to data processed by YouTube.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party entities that assist in app analytics, hosting, or customer support, under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Obligations: Where disclosure is required by law, regulation, or legal process.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You can update or delete your account information at any time within the app settings.

You can revoke app permissions, such as location or storage, through your device settings.
