Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

At AEO App, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your details when you use the AEO App.

We may collect the following types of information when you use the AEO App:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, or login credentials provided during registration.
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, device type, operating system version, and app activity data to improve user experience.
  • Permissions (if applicable): Camera, storage, or location access, only with user approval.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the features of the AEO App.
  • To create and manage your user account.
  • To send important updates, notifications, and customer support responses.
  • To improve app performance, security, and user experience.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party entities that assist in app analytics, hosting, or customer support, under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Obligations: Where disclosure is required by law, regulation, or legal process.

5. Your Rights & Choices

You can update or delete your account information at any time within the app settings.

You can revoke app permissions, such as location or storage, through your device settings.

6. Children’s Privacy

The AEO App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so that we can take appropriate steps to remove the information.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted within the app along with the revised Effective Date.
