Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 12, 2026

AEO-APP.com, operated by AEO-APP PTE. LTD., values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, access, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use our website and AI-powered marketing platform.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you register for or use AEO-APP.com.

1.1 Personal Information

  • Name and email address.
  • Phone number and business contact information.
  • Account credentials and authentication information.
  • Company name, website URL, and business information.

1.2 Device and Usage Information

  • IP address and browser information.
  • Device type and operating system.
  • Application activity and usage logs.
  • Diagnostic information used to improve platform performance.

1.3 Google and YouTube Account Information

When you choose to connect your Google account or YouTube channel to AEO-APP.com, we use Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization process to request access to information required to provide the requested YouTube integration features.

Depending on the permissions you authorize, we may access:

  • YouTube channel identifiers and channel information.
  • Channel names and relevant channel details.
  • Information required to upload videos and YouTube Shorts.
  • Video titles, descriptions, and publishing information.
  • Video upload and publishing status.
  • Information necessary to manage authorized publishing workflows.
  • AEO-APP.com uses YouTube API Services. By connecting your YouTube account, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

We only access information permitted by the OAuth permissions you explicitly authorize through Google's consent screen. Connecting your YouTube account is optional.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage customer accounts.
  • To provide AI-powered SEO and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) analysis.
  • To generate marketing content, blog articles, and content strategies.
  • To generate social media calendars, marketing posters, and videos.
  • To connect authorized social media accounts.
  • To provide YouTube video upload and publishing functionality.
  • To schedule and manage customer-approved social media content.
  • To process subscriptions and payments.
  • To improve application performance, functionality, and security.
  • To provide customer support and service notifications.

2.1 How We Use YouTube Data

When you connect your YouTube channel, AEO-APP.com uses authorized YouTube data only to provide the YouTube-related functionality requested by you.

This includes:

  • Identifying the YouTube channel you have authorized.
  • Uploading videos and YouTube Shorts when you initiate or authorize publishing.
  • Managing video titles, descriptions, and publishing information.
  • Supporting video scheduling and publishing workflows.
  • Displaying relevant upload and publishing statuses within your dashboard.

AEO-APP.com does not independently publish videos without user authorization or an explicitly configured publishing instruction.

We do not sell YouTube user data, use it for unrelated advertising, or use it to create independent advertising profiles.

3. Google API Services and Limited Use

AEO-APP.com's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We use Google user data only to provide or improve user-facing features that are relevant to the permissions granted by the user.
  • We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising purposes.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as permitted by Google's policies, including where necessary to provide requested services or comply with applicable legal requirements.
  • Access to Google user data is restricted to authorized personnel and service providers who need such access to provide the requested functionality.
  • We do not use Google user data for purposes unrelated to the features disclosed in this Privacy Policy.
  • We do not use Google user data for personalized advertising or sell it to data brokers.

4. Data Protection and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect personal information and authorized account data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

Our security practices include appropriate measures such as:

  • Secure HTTPS communication.
  • Authentication and access control mechanisms.
  • Restricted access to customer information.
  • Secure handling of OAuth access and refresh tokens.
  • Cloud infrastructure security measures.
  • Monitoring and security practices to help prevent unauthorized access.

OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are used only to maintain authorized connections and perform the services requested by customers.

We do not request or store Google account passwords. Authentication is handled through Google's official OAuth authorization process.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or Google user data.

We may use trusted third-party service providers to operate AEO-APP.com, including:

  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • AI service providers for requested content generation.
  • Payment processing providers.
  • Application monitoring and technical support providers.
  • Social media platforms and APIs when customers authorize integrations.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their respective services and subject to appropriate contractual and security obligations.

We do not permit third parties to use Google user data for their own advertising or unrelated purposes.

We may disclose information where required by applicable laws, regulations, or valid legal requests.

6. YouTube Account Disconnection and Data Deletion

Customers remain in control of their connected YouTube accounts. You can disconnect your YouTube channel through the account integration settings in AEO-APP.com, where available.

You may also revoke AEO-APP.com's access directly through your Google Account permissions:

Manage Google Account Permissions

When you disconnect your YouTube account:

  • We stop using the revoked authorization to access your YouTube account.
  • We remove or disable the associated OAuth credentials according to our data retention procedures.
  • We stop performing further authorized YouTube actions.

Disconnecting your YouTube account does not automatically delete your AEO-APP.com customer account.

To request deletion of stored YouTube-related information, including associated authorization credentials, contact us using the email address provided in the Contact Us section.

Please include your registered email address and identify the YouTube integration or information you want deleted. We will process deletion requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements and our data retention obligations.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain customer accounts, fulfill contractual obligations, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

YouTube-related information and OAuth credentials are retained only as necessary to support the authorized integration and requested functionality.

When information is no longer required, we take appropriate steps to delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable legal and security obligations.
