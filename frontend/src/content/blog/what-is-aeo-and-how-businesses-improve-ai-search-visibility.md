# What Is AEO and How Can Businesses Improve Their AI Search Visibility? 12 Questions Answered

**AEO**, or **Answer Engine Optimization**, is the work of getting your business named in the answers that **ChatGPT**, **Perplexity**, **Gemini**, and **Google AI Overviews** provide. A normal search shows a list of links. An AI answer gives one written reply and mentions a few sources. **AEO** is about being one of those sources.

For Singapore businesses, this matters because more people now ask an AI first and open a search page later, if at all. If your brand is missing from the reply, the customer may never hear of you.

Our app is built for this job. It shows where you stand in **AI answers**, who is ahead of you, and what to publish next. Here are the questions we hear most.

## 1. What is the difference between AEO and SEO?

**SEO** is about ranking. You want your page high in Google search results. **AEO** is about being mentioned or linked inside the answer an AI writes.

You can sit on page one of Google and still be missing from an AI answer. So, ranking alone tells you very little.

That is why our app doesn't just show you a rank. It works with **search prompts**, the actual sentences people type, like “AI search engine optimization tools Singapore”. For each prompt, you see whether you are cited, mentioned without a link, or absent.

The question shifts from “where do I rank?” to “when someone asks this, am I in the answer?”

For a deeper comparison, read our guide to [**AEO vs SEO vs GEO: The Complete Guide**](https://aeo-app.com/blog/aeo-vs-seo-vs-geo-the-complete-guide/).

## 2. Which AI answer engines should I optimize for in Singapore?

Our app tracks four: **ChatGPT**, **Perplexity**, **Gemini**, and **Google AI Overviews**. Every prompt row on the dashboard shows how you appear on each one.

Why all four? Because they do not agree. The same prompt can show your brand first on one engine and not at all on another. If you only check ChatGPT, you might think everything is fine, or everything is broken, and both could be wrong.

Pick the **AI search engines** your customers use most, but look at all four side by side so you are not guessing.

## 3. What content changes do I need to make for AEO?

Start with the answer. If someone asks “how do I track my brand in ChatGPT?”, your page should answer that in the first lines, then explain. Use clear headings and short paragraphs, and cover the questions that sit next to your main one.

Our app helps through **Blog Topics**. For every prompt you pick, it suggests a set of ideas: a pillar guide, an FAQ, a case study, an explainer, a checklist, and a comparison. You can generate a draft for any of them, read it, and approve it. Nothing goes out until you say so.

You can check by hand. Open each engine, ask the questions your customers ask, and see if you appear.

## 5. What technical fixes improve AEO performance?

Some fixes live on your website: **structured data**, a current sitemap, working links, **HTTPS**, and fast page load speed. Your developer or website platform handles those.

Our app does not edit your website’s code; it reads your site to build your **Company Profile**, and you can scan again whenever the site changes. It also has a **Domain Authority** section that shows which prompts you're missing.

That tells you where to look first. Technical fixes help engines read you. Content decides whether they quote you. You need both.

## 6. How long does it take to see AEO results?

We will not give a fixed number of weeks, because it depends on how crowded your prompts are, how strong your site is, and how often you publish.

What we can offer is a way to measure it. Run the analysis once for your starting point. Publish. Run it again. Our app shows what changed since the last run so you can compare it against your own numbers.

## 7. What does the AI Visibility Score tell me?

It is a score out of 100 that sums up how visible you are across the four engines for your tracked prompts. Think of it as the one number to watch from run to run.

It does not stand alone. Next to it, you get **Share of Voice**, which shows how you compare with competitors, prompts with presence, and cited answers. The score shows how you are doing overall, and the other numbers explain why.

**Cited answers** counts how many of the answers across your prompts and engines actually cite you. That is the number closest to what **AEO** is after.

## 8. How do I choose which prompts to track?

Start with what your customers would really ask. Our app gives you a Top list in three sizes: **Top 5, Top 10, and Top 20**, with user intent and monthly search volume beside each prompt.

If a question matters to your business and isn't on the list, add it yourself with **Add Prompt**. It is tracked next to the Top list and analyzed on the next run.

A simple way to choose: look at the volume, look at where you are absent, and pick the prompts where winning would bring real customers.

## 9. How do I see which competitors are ahead of me in AI answers?

Open the **Competitors** section. Our app lists your competitors with their domains and their **Share of Voice** for your prompts, and you can open the full list with **View All**.

This matters because AI answers have limited room. Only a handful of brands get mentioned, so every mention a competitor gets is one you didn't. Seeing their share next to yours shows how big the gap is and whether it is closing after each run.
