# Does Automated Blog Posting Help You Rank in ChatGPT?

Automated blog posting means using a tool to publish blog posts on a schedule, without checking which questions those blogs answer. It saves time, but it does not automatically make your business visible on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, or Google AI Overviews. These AI search engines don't recommend businesses just because they publish content frequently. What matters is whether your content answers the specific questions people ask. They also consider the information available across your website and other online platforms.

AI tools can also differ in the brands they recommend. Understanding how ChatGPT and Claude cite different brands helps businesses understand why their visibility may vary across AI platforms.

This is where AEO-APP helps. It connects your published content to the questions people ask AI tools and lets you track whether your business appears in their answers.

## The Problem With Posting Without Purpose

An automated blog posting tool can keep your blog schedule full every week. But that does not necessarily mean your content will help your business appear in AI search. What matters is the information each blog post provides.

When a customer asks ChatGPT or Perplexity to recommend a business like yours, the AI engine looks for content that answers that particular question. A well-written blog on a general topic may not help much if it doesn't address what the customer is looking for.

This explains why some businesses publish blogs regularly, but have very little AI search visibility. They keep publishing blogs, but those blogs may not relate to the questions people ask AI tools.

The better approach is to understand what your customers are searching for first. Once you know their common questions, you can create blog posts that answer them.

## Why AI Search Engines Need Evidence of Expertise

AI engines understand your business by looking at publicly available information. This includes your website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn page, and social media channels.

If these platforms describe your business differently, AI engines may struggle to understand what services you provide and who you serve. Consistent information across all these channels gives them a clearer picture.

Your website's authority also matters. Domain authority is an SEO metric that evaluates a website's ranking potential based on factors such as backlinks. Although it is not a direct Google ranking factor, it is commonly used to understand a website's overall authority.

You should also consider some technical aspects. Structured data such as Organization schema and FAQPage schema helps search engines understand what your business does and which questions your website pages answer.

An llms.txt file can guide AI tools to important information on your website. Meanwhile, robots.txt tells web crawlers, including AI crawlers, which parts of your website they can access. You can learn how to check if your website is invisible to AI crawlers to identify potential access and visibility issues.

Even if your business has the right experience and expertise, AI engines may not recognise it if they cannot access the relevant information.

You can also examine your website's server logs to understand whether AI crawlers are visiting and accessing your pages. Learn how to identify AI crawler traffic through server logs.
